A Little Bit About Me

Hi! My name is Jonathan Sluka, and I am a high school senior looking to study music technology as a major. I have a passion for combining my creative skills with my technical skills. My DAW of choice is Ableton Live. I believe my greatest strength is mixing and mastering audio recordings.

My Projects

Here are some of my recent projects I have been working on.

Audition Piece: "Breezin'" by George Benson

Description: Recorded and mixed a video and audio of myself playing the jazz track "Breezin'" by hometown hero and jazz guitar legend George Benson. I have listened to George Benson for years, but I had never attempted a song of his on guitar before this recording. I have a great deal of respect for him and his influence on the world of jazz music. I hope you enjoy my rendition of this incredible song.

Audition Piece: "Midnight Rendezvous" by Casiopea

Description: Recorded and mixed a video of myself playing a song by highly influential Japanese jazz fusion band Casiopea. I have always had a great deal of interest in foreign music, specifically Japanese music. Through this interest, I found Casiopea and quickly fell in love with their ability to combine jazz and rock in such a tasteful way. This particular track is a favorite of mine, and I hope that if you did not know of this band before you saw this that you might consider checking them out!

Audition Piece: "I'm Still Cheering for the 1980 U.S. Hockey Team" by Oakwood

Description: This song has been a recent favorite of mine and is more in line with what I usually listen to every day. I got started playing guitar because of punk rock bands, so I felt it only fitting that I include an offshoot of punk rock in my portfolio somewhere. I would describe this song as a combination of screamo and Midwest emo. I love that bands like Oakwood are proving the common misconception that punk rock and its many subgenres are "simple" to be false. The use of a non-conventional tuning is also very interesting, and adds to the song's complexity. I am very proud of my recording of this song, and I hope that you can find the same beauty in this song that I do.
